An AI proposal writer can help organise a brief into a first draft. You still need to decide what you will deliver, what you will charge and which promises you can keep.

I build SoloPad, which includes proposal and contract drafting. This guide describes a workflow you can try. It does not claim a measured improvement in conversion rates or a guaranteed time saving.

## Give the draft useful inputs

Prepare a short brief with the client's objective, deliverables, exclusions, deadline, price and payment schedule. Add relevant experience you can verify. Leave out private client information that is unnecessary for the draft.

For example, an **illustrative project brief** could be:

> Draft a proposal for a five-page website for a dental clinic. Include a booking-tool integration and contact form. The agreed price is £2,000, with a 50% deposit. Delivery is three weeks after the client supplies approved copy. Exclude logo design and ongoing maintenance. Include two revision rounds.

Use your actual agreed details in a real proposal. If something is unknown, ask the client or mark it for discussion rather than letting the model choose it.

## Review every commercial commitment

Check the scope against the original enquiry. A draft can add a deliverable that sounds helpful but creates unpaid work. Read dates and dependencies together: a deadline may depend on the client providing content or access.

Confirm the price, currency, taxes and payment milestones. Review revision limits, ownership wording and acceptance steps. Keep the language clear enough that the client can explain what they are buying.

Check every case study and result. Use only work you actually completed and outcomes you can support. Remove invented testimonials, client names and performance figures.

## Edit it into your voice

Keep the client's problem near the top. Explain your approach in a few concrete sentences, then give the scope, timeline, price and next step.

Read the draft aloud. Replace phrases you would not use in a client conversation. Our [freelance proposal guide](/blog/how-to-write-a-freelance-proposal) and [one-page template](/blog/one-page-freelance-proposal-template-uk) provide a structure to check against.

## Drafting and sending are separate steps

A general writing assistant can produce text that you paste into your document tool. Check its current plan limits and terms before using it. Avoid treating every AI service as free or unlimited.

An integrated proposal tool can keep drafting and document preparation together. Test what happens after generation: editing, client delivery, acceptance and export all matter.
