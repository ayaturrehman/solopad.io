**HoneyBook vs Dubsado comes down to the client workflow you need and the plan that includes it.** Bring Bonsai into the comparison if tracking project time alongside billing is a priority. Compare the cost of your complete workflow before choosing by the advertised starting price.

I build SoloPad, a competing freelance management tool. This is a comparison of published plans, with a checklist you can use in your own trials. It is not an independent product test.

*Pricing and plan details checked on 27 October 2026. USD figures below come from the vendors' US pricing pages; SoloPad prices are in GBP. These are not currency-converted equivalents.*

## HoneyBook vs Dubsado vs Bonsai: pricing at a glance

| HoneyBook Starter | $29/month, billed yearly | Includes proposals, contracts, payments and a client portal. Check higher tiers for scheduling and automation. |

Annual billing commits you to a different payment schedule from a rolling monthly subscription. Include seat charges, payment fees, applicable taxes and exchange costs in your budget. Treat introductory discounts separately from the renewal price.

## HoneyBook: check the client document and payment experience

HoneyBook's published Starter plan bundles client documents, payment collection and a portal. Its pricing page also lists HoneyBook AI, so the claim that HoneyBook has no AI features is outdated. Essentials adds scheduling and automations. The page currently advertises a 30-day trial. [See HoneyBook's plan details](https://www.honeybook.com/pricing).

During a trial, build a proposal that resembles a real job. Add your own scope, revision allowance and payment milestones. Open the client link on your phone and check how clearly it explains the next step.

A polished default template is useful. Your decision should also account for how quickly you can edit it when a client asks for a change.

## Dubsado: check which workflows require Premier

Dubsado separates its Starter and Premier plans. Its published comparison puts scheduling, automated workflows, public proposals and Zapier integration in Premier. It now advertises a 21-day Premier trial; the old description of an ongoing three-client free plan should not guide a new purchase. [See Dubsado's current plans](https://www.dubsado.com/pricing).

Write down your ideal process before configuring automation. For example: enquiry, qualification form, discovery call, proposal, signed agreement and deposit. Mark the steps that need your judgement and the steps you want the system to handle.

Then test one full journey. Count the manual interventions and check what happens when a client changes their mind halfway through. That gives you more useful evidence than the number of automation options in a feature list.

## Bonsai: compare Essentials when you need billing

Bonsai Basic covers time tracking, project tasks and CRM. Essentials is the relevant starting point when you also need invoices and contracts. Pricing is per user, so calculate the total for everyone who needs access. [See Bonsai's plan comparison](https://www.hellobonsai.com/pricing).

For hourly work, test the route from a time entry to an invoice. For fixed-fee work, check deposits and milestones. If you work on retainers, try a realistic monthly allowance using your own [timesheet template](/blog/timesheet-template-uk).

## A practical trial checklist

Use a sample client and project in each tool:

1. Add the contact and create an enquiry or project.
2. Draft a proposal with deliverables, exclusions and a clear price. Our [one-page proposal template](/blog/one-page-freelance-proposal-template-uk) gives you a common starting point.
3. Check the contract and signature process using your own [contract checklist](/blog/freelance-contract-template-guide).
4. Prepare a deposit invoice, then check how the remaining balance would be billed.
5. View everything as the client on a phone.
6. Check reminders, exports, cancellation terms and the full recurring cost.

Record what took effort and what worked well. Choose the tool that handles your regular work at a cost you can sustain.
