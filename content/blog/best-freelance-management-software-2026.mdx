The best freelance management software is the tool that handles your regular client work at a cost you can sustain. Compare the plan that includes your required features, rather than each product's lowest advertised price.

I build SoloPad, one of the products below. This shortlist uses published vendor information, with source links so you can check it yourself. It does not represent an independent hands-on test or measured ranking.

## A shortlist by workflow

| --- | --- | --- |

Check current checkout terms before subscribing. USD and GBP prices are not converted equivalents. Include tax, processing, seat and usage charges in your budget.

Hectic is Moxie's former name, rather than a seventh separate product. HoneyBook, Moxie and Plutio advertise AI features. Test the particular drafting or automation task you need.

## Start with the work you do every week

Write down the complete journey from enquiry to final payment. Include the steps that get awkward: a revised scope, a client who needs a deposit invoice, a late approval or a request for an export.

For fixed-fee projects, proposal editing, signatures and staged billing may matter most. For hourly work, check time reports and the process for preparing an invoice. For recurring clients, check recurring billing and reminders on the plan you intend to buy.

Use our [proposal template](/blog/one-page-freelance-proposal-template-uk) and [invoice examples](/blog/freelance-invoice-template-examples) as common sample documents. The same input makes differences between tools easier to assess.

## Compare the full recurring cost

A per-user subscription can grow with your team. An annual monthly equivalent requires an upfront commitment. A low base price can also have payment or usage fees.

SoloPad's platform fee on client payments is 3% on Starter, 2% on Solo and 1% on Pro, plus Stripe processing fees. The subscription price alone does not describe the full cost.

Keep specialist accounting software if your accountant or reporting obligations require it. A client management tool should not be assumed to replace tax filing or bookkeeping services.

## Test before migrating

Create a sample client, proposal, contract and invoice in your shortlisted tools. View the client journey on a phone. Check editing, exports, permissions and cancellation terms before moving real records.
